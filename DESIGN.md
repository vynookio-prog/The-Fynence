# Fynence Visual Direction

Reading this as: a personal finance and trading workspace for an individual operator, in an editorial luxury ledger style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- **Palette:** Deep Ink, Warm Cream, and Soft Paper establish a calm ledger-like foundation; Rust Orange is reserved for direct action and risk, while Olive and Vintage Gold distinguish positive and cautionary financial states.
- **Background:** the restrained cream, gold, rust, and olive light fields give depth to the workspace and reference aged paper and metallic accounting tools rather than a startup gradient.
- **Typography:** Playfair Display handles display moments to evoke a financial journal; DM Mono remains for values, filters, and system metadata where alignment aids scanning.
- **Surfaces:** shared cards are translucent paper surfaces with an inset highlight, not pervasive frosted glass; blur is reserved for the fixed navigation layers that need to stay legible over changing content.
- **Spacing and shape:** generous outer spacing gives charts and financial data room to breathe; cards use a moderate 18px radius while primary actions alone use rounded forms.
- **Motion:** hover lift signals a selectable panel and color transitions confirm state changes. No ambient or looping decorative motion is introduced.
